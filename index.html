HOW TO PLAY

🎯 OBJECTIVE

Deflect the XB balls with your beam bat to shatter bricks! Destroy all breakable bricks on the board to clear the wave and earn a +500 wave bonus.

BRICK SKILL & TYPE EFFECT
BUFF OVERDRIVE Activate Yo's beam bat and extended the bat for 30 seconds. Timer refreshes on repeat hits.
BUFF IROHA BALL Gives magnet effect to the nearest brick, drawing the ball that breaks the brick straight into it.
BUFF BROKEN MORAL Explodes and destroys all adjacent bricks in a 3x3 radius.
BUFF DEFLECT SERVER Deploys a safety shield beneath the bat for 15s. Destroyed after 1 hit.
BUFF LUMINOUS CRYSTAL Summoned luminous crystal that fire upward plasma lasers whenever a ball bounces on the bat for 30s.
BUFF POISON RAIN Poisons 1 adjacent brick. Poison destroys the brick after 5s or cascades to another unhit brick.
BUFF IGNITION Requires 3 hits. On 3rd hit, detonates diagonal X-line laser beams across the entire grid!
BUFF ROAR OF OUTRAGE Requires 3 hits. On 3rd hit, detonates cross-fire laser beams along its entire row and column!
BUFF TIME TO EAT Cleanses active debuffs.
BUFF PLAY BALL Drops extra XB Ball.
BUFF MONEY IS POWER! Drops kane. Earn up to 1000 points when collected.
BUFF FIGHTING SPIRIT Turns ball into a blazing Fire Ball that pierces through bricks without bouncing back for 5 consecutive hits!
BUFF TWILIGHT'S EMBRACE Activates a gravity field in the bottom of playfield for 8s. Balls will slow down as they fall.
BUFF ENERGIZE Accelerates bat movement speed by +75% for 20s.
BUFF HEAVY METAL Leaps across the field and explodes 5-brick cross on landing!
BUFF INSTANT PERCEPTION Fires 5 sequential BB shots from the bat straight up that pierces through bricks.
BUFF BREAKTHROUGH Summons a wind updraft from the bottom for 1s that bounces every falling ball upward!
BUFF ITS YOUR CUTE ZERO! Drops a Zero Ball! Catch it to earn +1 Extra Life.
BUFF POWER RESONANCE Slow motion for 2 seconds, then resets all ball speed.
DEBUFF POWER HARASSMENT Clamps all points gained to strictly 1 point for 60s.
DEBUFF TOKIMEKI TIME Breaks the bat to half for 60 seconds.
DEBUFF LEBENSTRIEB Drops red shipping containers. Catching one slows the bat for 2s!
DEBUFF REJECTION IMPULSE Floods the brick field with livestream comments for 30s.
DEBUFF HEAVEN OR HELL Turns all other bricks into fake Ruriiro. Remove by finding the Real Ruriiro. All bricks effect will remain as normal bricks until reverted.
